ANET: Sell-the-News, Not Sell-the-Category

Arista Networks delivered a genuinely strong Q1 2026 — revenue of $2.71B (+35.1% YoY) beat consensus of $2.61B, EPS of $0.87 beat $0.79 estimates by 10%, and billings growth accelerated to 54% YoY. Yet the stock collapsed ~17% in five sessions. The culprit was Q2 revenue guidance of $2.8B and a full-year raise to $11.5B that fell short of "lofty expectations" baked in after a near-90% prior-year rally. Management flagged multi-year switch silicon supply constraints (TSMC capacity limited) and gross margin compression from product mix — both well-understood issues, not new demand problems.

Morningstar raised its fair value to $190 (from $175) post-selloff, calling the fundamentals "excellent" and the drop a reset opportunity; TD Cowen raised its target to $200 from $170 citing hyperscale capex commentary. At ~$141.77, ANET sits well below its 52-week high of ~$179.80. Notably, ANET raised its AI fabrics revenue target to $3.5B and full-year growth guidance to 27.7% — underlying category demand is not topping. The new XPO high-density liquid-cooled pluggable optics platform, designed to reduce networking rack count by up to 75% in AI data centers, is a new product catalyst embedded in this quarter's results.

The read-through: this is a single-name valuation reset, and the broader optical networking category remains structurally sound.

The Gradual-Intensity Setups With Runway: CIEN and MRVL

Ciena (CIEN) is the cleaner near-term setup. Q1 FY2026 EPS of $1.35 beat analyst estimates of $1.05 by 29%; revenue of $1.43B (+33% YoY) was a company record. Backlog surged to ~$7B, up $2B in a single quarter, with management noting nearly all new orders now slot for FY2027 fulfillment — a visibility cushion most optical names lack. FY2026 guidance was raised to $5.9B–$6.3B (implying ~27% YoY growth at midpoint), with forward consensus at $6.17B for FY2026 and $7.51B for FY2027. Operating margin expanded to 13.4% from 7.7% a year ago.

On the product front, Ciena introduced the Vesta 206.4T CPO optical engine — the industry's first high-density open-ecosystem pluggable CPO solution — demoed at OFC 2026 with samples targeted for Q2 2026. Its WaveLogic 6 Extreme 1.6T quantum-safe platform is already shipping, and a Coherent-Lite 1.6T pluggable transceiver (3nm CMOS) is in active hyperscaler deployment. Three hyperscalers have selected CIEN's optical solutions for AI "scale-across" training cluster architectures. CIEN's 30-day price change stands at +10.5%; 90-day at +89.2%. Analyst targets from BofA ($355), TD Cowen ($425), and Stifel ($430) are meaningfully below the current ~$548 price, which is a caution flag on near-term sell-side runway — though CIEN's S&P 500 re-entry in February 2026 triggered an estimated $9–15B in forced passive buying, compressing its discount. Next earnings June 4, 2026, is the next catalyst gate. Risk to monitor: CEO Gary Smith has sold 139,153 shares ($31.5M) with 37 insider sales and zero purchases in the past six months.

Marvell (MRVL) shows the strongest demand revision cadence in the cohort. MRVL has raised its FY2027 revenue forecast three consecutive times in six months — from $9.5B in September to ~$11B — with a $15B FY2028 target now in view. Data center revenue crossed $6B in FY2026 (+46% YoY), with 40% growth guided for FY2027. At Q4 FY2026 earnings, MRVL announced the industry's first secure 1.6T ZR and ZR+ DCI modules powered by a 2nm coherent DSP. The Celestial AI acquisition targets $500M annualized CPO revenue by Q4 FY2028 and $1B by 2029, with Photonic Fabric high-volume shipments beginning next year. MRVL's 30-day move is +32.4% (90-day +106.6%), approaching the upper bound of the gradual-intensity window.

The Macro Backdrop: 1.6T Demand Is Real and Accelerating

TrendForce projects global 800G+ transceiver shipments to leap from 24M units in 2025 to nearly 63M in 2026 — a 2.6x jump in a single year — with the high-speed datacom optics market growing from $9B in 2024 toward $17B by 2026. Broadcom and Marvell are the dual primary beneficiaries of both DSP supply and CPO qualification, with TSMC silicon supply capacity as the primary near-term governor. Ciena and Marvell also dominate the merchant coherent DCI pluggable modem stack (400ZR/ZR+), with LightCounting identifying Marvell and Cisco/Acacia as market leaders and Ciena as the primary third supplier — an oligopoly structure that insulates both from commoditization pressure in the near term.

Rotating Leaders, Differentiated Laggards

The parabolic names — AAOI (-17.5% in 5 days after a +210.9% 90-day run), LITE (-9.1% 5-day, +56.6% 90-day), and CRDO (+57.6% in 30 days, now at consensus target ~$188.92 vs. spot ~$188.50) — are clearly rotating. CRDO's 800G ZF optical transceivers are shipping to customers like TensorWay with strong execution, but sell-side runway at current prices is effectively exhausted.

Fabrinet (FN) reported record Q2 FY2026 revenue of $1.133B (+35.9% YoY), but its stock is -6.2% over 30 days and -5.9% over 5 days on tariff concerns — most of FN's optical production is in Thailand, facing 19% tariffs reduced from 36%, creating a structural cost disadvantage vs. peers expanding U.S. manufacturing. FN looks more like a supply-chain risk story than a share-gain play at this stage. Broadcom (AVGO) is +15.7% in 30 days with consensus targets at $451–$458 implying only ~5–6% additional upside from ~$430 — a modest buffer. COHR is showing a clean gradual profile (+9.0% 30-day, +38.3% 90-day, -0.1% 5-day), though it has been covered previously in this category.
